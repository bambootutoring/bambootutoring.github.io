About The Founder

Hi, I'm Sophie, the founder of Bamboo STEM Tutoring. I created this tutoring service with one clear goal: to make learning accessible, confidence-building, and genuinely enjoyable for every student. I hold an MChem degree and am currently pursuing a PhD in Chemistry, which allows me to bring both strong subject expertise and real research experience into my teaching. I also hold a level 2 certificate in special educational needs and disabilities.

Over the years, I've supported students across GCSE, A-level, and Functional Skills courses, helping them break down difficult topics into clear, manageable steps. My approach is calm, structured, and fully tailored to each learner—because everyone learns differently, and the right explanation can change everything.

Through Bamboo STEM Tutoring, I combine my academic background with a passion for helping students grow. Whether you're building core maths skills, tackling tricky chemistry concepts, or preparing for exams, my focus is on clear explanations, steady progress, and building lasting confidence.

If you're looking for expert, friendly support from a tutor who is genuinely invested in your success, you're in the right place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make high-quality tutoring accessible to all students, regardless of background or learning differences. We are passionate about providing inclusive, affordable tuition that is carefully tailored to each learner’s individual needs, including those with special educational needs. Through patience, understanding, and personalized teaching strategies, we aim to build confidence, remove barriers to learning, and help every student thrive academically and personally.
